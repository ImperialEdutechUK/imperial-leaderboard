# Manager's guide

For Sadeev and the other department managers. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## The weekly routine — about two minutes

1. Download the weekly report from Screenshot Monitor as you do now.
2. Go to the site → **Manager sign-in** → **Upload a week**.
3. Drag the file onto the page.
4. Check the preview:
   - a green bar confirms the totals match the ones printed on the report;
   - the department and the week dates are filled in automatically;
   - every person should say **Matched**.
5. If the week contained a bank holiday, set **Target hours override** (e.g. `28`
   for a four-day week) and add a note like *"Easter Monday — 4-day week"*.
6. Press **Import as draft**, look at the standings, then press **Publish**.

That's it. The leaderboard, badges, streaks and monthly totals all update themselves.

> **Nothing is saved until you press Import**, and nothing is visible to staff
> until you press Publish. You can always start again.

---

## Reading the preview screen

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Matched** (green) | Recognised from a previous week | Nothing |
| **Close match — check** (amber) | Similar to someone on the roster, e.g. a typo | Confirm it's the right person, or pick someone else from the dropdown |
| **New person** (blue) | Nobody on the roster matches | Fine for a genuine new starter. If it's an existing person spelled differently, choose their name from the dropdown — the system remembers that spelling from then on |

## The score, in plain English

Everyone's weekly score is out of about 1000 points, split evenly:

- **Half from hours** — measured against the weekly target. Full credit arrives
  slightly above target and then stops. Working 50 hours does not beat working 38.
- **Half from activity %** — Screenshot Monitor's own figure.

Plus bonuses: **+20** for hitting target, **+25** for activity of 85% or more,
**+25** for beating your own previous best.

Anyone can tap a score on the public page and see the exact sum. That is
deliberate — people trust a leaderboard they can check.

### Why hours are capped

Without a cap, the way to win is simply to log the most hours. That rewards long
days rather than good work, and it invites people to leave the tracker running.
The cap means the hours half is essentially "did you do a full week", and the
activity half is where the competition actually happens.

### People on leave

Anyone under the qualifying threshold (8 hours by default) is shown with a dash
instead of a position, not placed last. Annual leave should never look like poor
performance.

## Prizes

**Prizes → pick the month → confirm the champion.** The leader is pre-selected
but the choice is yours — you might reward someone's improvement rather than
their raw total. Add the prize as free text ("£50 voucher") and it appears on the
Hall of Fame permanently.

Monthly totals are the sum of weekly points. A week belongs to whichever month
contains its Thursday, so a week spanning two months is counted once.

## Fixing things

| Situation | Where to go |
|---|---|
| Wrong hours or activity for one person | **Weeks → open the week → click their row** |
| Published too early | **Weeks → open the week → Unpublish** |
| Same person appears twice | **Roster → open the duplicate → Merge into someone else** |
| Someone left | **Roster → uncheck Active** (their history is kept) |
| You'd rather not compete against your own team | **Roster → your own entry → Hide from the leaderboard** |
| Everyone's score looks too similar | **Scoring → raise the weekly target** |

## Tuning the scoring

**Scoring** has a live preview: drag the balance slider and the sample table
re-ranks instantly, so you can see the consequences before saving.

Things worth knowing:

- **If everyone exceeds the target**, the hours half becomes the same number for
  everyone and the ranking is decided purely by activity. The app warns you when
  this happens. Raising the target restores the spread.
- **"Apply to weeks already published"** re-scores history. Positions people have
  already celebrated can change. Leave it off unless you are correcting a real
  mistake.
- Each department can have its own settings. Sales and Course Development do not
  have to be judged the same way.

## Rolling it out to the other departments

1. **Admin → Departments** — check each department's **code**. This is the bit in
   brackets after a name in Screenshot Monitor, like `Aaisha (CDD)`. Getting it
   right means uploads route themselves; getting it wrong just means the manager
   picks the department from a dropdown.
2. **Admin → Managers** — create an account for each department head. They can
   only see and manage their own department.
3. Send each team their department URL. No password needed to look.

## A word about activity %

Activity % measures keyboard and mouse input during tracked time. It is a decent
proxy for hands-on work and a poor proxy for value.

A course developer editing video will score high. Someone spending the week
reading, planning, in meetings or on calls will score low while doing excellent
work. Two departments doing different kinds of work cannot be compared fairly on
this number alone.

This caveat is printed at the bottom of every public page. Please keep it there,
and please say the same thing out loud when you launch it — the leaderboard works
best as something people enjoy, and that depends on everyone understanding what
it does and doesn't measure.

Occasionally you may see someone at or near 100% activity every week. The system
flags this for you privately. It can be entirely genuine — sustained data entry
or editing produces exactly that pattern — or it can indicate a mouse-jiggler.
The app does not guess, and neither should the leaderboard; it is a prompt for a
quiet conversation, not evidence.
